    Back-of-the-Envelope Estimation

    6 sections · 347 words

    Sizing an Instagram-scale system on paper before designing it.

    1. What We Estimate

    • QPS (Queries Per Second) - the request rate.
    • Storage unit - how much data accumulates, estimated over 5 years.
    • Read operations are SELECT; write operations are INSERT, DELETE, and UPDATE.

    2. Power of Two

    Power Scale Unit
    10 thousands kilobyte
    20 millions megabyte
    30 billions gigabyte
    40 trillions terabyte
    50 quadrillions petabyte
    60 - exabyte

    3. Assumptions (Instagram)

    1. Monthly active users: about 2 billion.
    2. 60% of these users use Instagram daily: about 1.2 billion.
    3. DAU (Daily Active Users): 1.2 billion.
    4. A user seeing the feed: 1 feed request = 1 query.
    5. A user posting a photo/video/reel: 1 post request = 1 query.
    6. A user checks the feed 30 times a day (average).
    7. Estimate horizon: 5 years.
    8. A user publishes one picture/reel a day (average).

    4. Feed View QPS

    QPS = DAU * 30 / 86,400
    = 1.2B * 30 / 86,400
    = ~420K/sec

Peak QPS = 2 * QPS
         = ~840K/sec

    5. Upload QPS

    QPS = 1.2B * 1 / 86,400
    = ~14K/sec

Peak QPS = 2 * QPS
         = ~28K/sec

    6. Storage Unit

    Assumptions:

    • 20% of users post video (50 MB).
    • 80% of users post photo (1 MB).

    Photos:

    photos/day = 1.2B * 80% = ~1B
1B * 1 MB  = 2^30 * 1 * 2^20 = 2^50 = ~1PB

    Videos:

    1.2B * 20% = ~0.25B
0.25B * 50MB = 2^50 * 12 = ~12PB

    Totals:

    Total per day = 12PB + 1PB = 13 PB

For 5 years:
13PB/day * 365 days * 5 years = ~24,000 PB
24 * 10^3 = 24 * 2^10 * 2^50 = 24 * 2^60 = ~24 EB (Exabyte)
