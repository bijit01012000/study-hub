    2026 Edition

    Prepare for the second question - the one that tests what you owned, what failed and why you chose this design over the alternative.

    The Premise

    You rarely lose the round on the first answer. You lose it when the interviewer asks why, what failed, and what did you personally do?

    Preparation Complete

    Close the book. Open your mouth.

    You are ready for the follow-up. Reading gives you the structure. Speaking turns it into an answer that sounds clear, specific, and genuinely yours.

    1. Choose your ten. Mark the questions most likely to expose a weak spot in your next interview.
    2. Answer out loud. Give yourself two minutes. Lead with the decision, show the trade-off, and finish with evidence.
    3. Practise the second question. Ask what failed, what you personally owned, and what you would change now. That is where strong candidates separate themselves.

    A polished answer can sound borrowed. A specific answer sounds true.
