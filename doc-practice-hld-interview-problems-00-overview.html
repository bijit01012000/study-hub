    High-Level Design Problems

    3 sections · 531 words

    Every problem across three levels — the shape, the scale, and the failure modes — built to be redrawn from memory.

    16 interview problems · 3 levels each · 51 pages

    How To Use This Set

    Each problem is one page per level:

    • Level 1 — The Shape (Foundation). Produce a coherent shape and defend three choices inside it. Depth is the next page — do not reach for it here.
    • Level 2 — The Defence (Intermediate). Defend the boxes you drew, with numbers, and name what breaks.
    • Level 3 — The Edges (Advanced). Walk the failure modes and the boundary conditions out loud.

    Use each sheet as revision: cover the right rail and the panels to its left, then walk the numbered path out loud. If you cannot say why a box is there and what breaks it, that box is your gap.
