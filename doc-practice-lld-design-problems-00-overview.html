    How Each Page Is Organised

    • The Ask — what the problem builds and what is explicitly out of scope.
    • Our Model — the actors, classes and interfaces, and the key operations with their guarantees.
    • The One Run to Hold in Your Head — a worked trace through the design.
    • The Change — the follow-up that adds a seam without reopening the design.
    • What the Picture Can't Show — the race or invariant a class diagram hides.
    • If They Make You Code It — the core methods in Java.
    • The Four Hazards — the concurrency and edge cases the interviewer probes.
    • The Three Lines They Are Watching — the exact code lines that carry the guarantee.
    • Rejection Signals — answers that end the interview.
    • Patterns Used — the design patterns the sheet leans on.
    • Say It Like This — the whole design in one spoken paragraph.
    • The Follow-ups That Decide It — eight questions with their answers.
    • Deliberately Not Built — the ghosts, and why they stay out.
    • This Sheet Also Answers — the other interview problems this design covers.

    That is the LLD Design Problems: 20 sheets, every one revision-ready.
